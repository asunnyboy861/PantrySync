Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add items to my pantry?

Tap the + button on the Pantry tab to manually add items, or use the barcode scanner to quickly look up products.

How does receipt scanning work?

Go to the Pantry tab and tap the barcode icon. Select "Scan Receipt" to take a photo of your receipt. The app will automatically detect and list items for you to add.

How do I sync data across devices?

Enable iCloud Sync in Settings. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID on all devices.

How do expiration alerts work?

The app sends notifications when items are close to expiring. You can set the alert threshold in Settings (default: 3 days before expiration).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, find PantrySync, and cancel. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Can I share my pantry with family members?

Yes! With iCloud Sync enabled and Family Sharing set up on your Apple ID, family members can access shared pantry data.
