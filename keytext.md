## Description

**PantrySync** is your all-in-one smart kitchen manager. Stop wasting food and start saving money with intelligent pantry tracking, smart grocery lists, and beautiful meal planning.

🧊 **Pantry Tracking**
• Track every item in your kitchen with expiration dates
• Get alerts before food expires so nothing goes to waste
• Organize by category and storage location (fridge, freezer, pantry, counter)
• Scan barcodes for instant product lookup with nutrition info

🛒 **Smart Grocery Lists**
• Create and organize shopping lists by category
• Auto-generate lists from your favorite recipes
• Check off items and move them directly to your pantry
• Track estimated prices for budgeting

📖 **Recipe Manager**
• Store your favorite recipes with full ingredient lists
• Add ingredients to your grocery list with one tap
• Track nutrition per serving (calories, protein, carbs, fat)
• Tag and favorite recipes for quick access

📅 **Meal Planning**
• Plan your week with a visual meal calendar
• Assign breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for each day
• Automatically generate grocery lists from your meal plan

📊 **Nutrition Tracking**
• Monitor daily calorie and macro intake
• Set personalized nutrition goals
• Beautiful charts showing weekly progress
• Track protein, carbs, and fat breakdown

📷 **Smart Scanning**
• Scan product barcodes for instant nutrition data from Open Food Facts
• Snap photos of receipts to automatically add items
• OCR-powered receipt parsing detects items, quantities, and prices

☁️ **iCloud Sync**
• Sync your data across all your Apple devices
• Share pantry data with family members
• Your data stays private in your iCloud account

Start reducing food waste and saving money today with PantrySync!
